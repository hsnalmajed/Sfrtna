Guideline 2.1 – answers:

1. Which companies provide the services?
Sfrtna does not sell or provide any travel service itself. It is a free trip-planning and price-comparison app (budget, destinations, day plans, guides). Flights, hotels, cars, tours and eSIMs are provided by established third-party travel companies (for example Expedia Group, Aviasales via Travelpayouts, Viator, and the airlines' own websites). When a user chooses an offer, the app opens that company's own website, where the booking and payment are completed under that company's terms. We never take payments or store payment details.

2. Relationship between Hassan Almajed and the providers?
Hassan Almajed is the independent developer and owner of Sfrtna. He has no ownership or employment relationship with these companies. Sfrtna is a registered publisher in their public affiliate programmes, which may pay a commission when a user books on their site, at no extra cost to the user.
