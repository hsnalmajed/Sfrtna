Guideline 5.1.2(i) – Privacy – Data Use and Sharing

The app does not track users, and it no longer collects any cookie used for tracking:
- The cookie prompt and Google Analytics have been removed from the iPhone app entirely.
- No third-party script, frame or tracking cookie runs inside the app. The app's web view blocks third-party scripts and frames and does not keep third-party cookies (WebKit content rules), and our release process checks this automatically before every update.
- Live flight and hotel listings from our partners, and every booking link, open on the partner's own website in a Safari sheet (SFSafariViewController), outside the app's web view.
- We have no advertising, do not share data with data brokers and do not link data with third-party data. App Privacy in App Store Connect states "Data Not Used to Track You".
Because the app does not track, it does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission. The behaviour is the same in every country and region.

To see it: open the app; no cookie prompt appears anywhere. Plan a trip, then search flights: "Live fares for your trip" lists the sites, and each one opens in a Safari sheet.

Guideline 2.1 – Information Needed

1. Which companies provide the services offered in the app?
Sfrtna does not sell or provide any travel service itself. It is a free trip-planning and comparison app (budget, destinations, day plans, guides). Flights, hotels, cars, tours and eSIMs are provided by established third-party travel companies, for example Expedia Group, Aviasales (through Travelpayouts), Stay22, Viator, and the airlines' own websites. When a user chooses an offer, the app opens that company's own website in a Safari sheet, where the booking and the payment are completed under that company's terms. We never take payments or store payment details.

2. What is the relationship between Hassan Almajed and the providers of these services?
Hassan Almajed is the independent developer and owner of Sfrtna. He has no ownership or employment relationship with these companies. Sfrtna is a registered publisher in their public affiliate programmes, which may pay a commission when a user books on their site, at no extra cost to the user.
