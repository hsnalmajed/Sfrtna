Hello, and thank you for the review.

We agreed with your feedback: build 27 opened on our website and felt like many travel search apps. We rebuilt the iPhone app around its own native screens and removed flight and hotel search from it. Answers to your questions:

**1. What does the app do?**
Sfrtna is a travel companion for Muslim families, before and during a trip. The app opens on a native "Today" screen: prayer times for where the traveller is, worked out on the device without internet; the next prayer with a countdown and optional alerts; the Hijri date; a qibla compass; and the nearest mosques and halal restaurants with walking directions. The native "Plan" tab asks a few questions (city, dates, who travels, what they enjoy) and builds a day-by-day trip plan that respects prayer times, Friday prayer, halal meals and children. "Keep on my phone" stores the plan on the device. It then opens fully offline in "My plans", with reminders before the trip, each morning's first stop, and prayer alerts in the visited city's own time zone.

**2. Who are the users?**
Arabic-speaking families from Saudi Arabia and the Gulf travelling abroad (Turkey, Malaysia, Sri Lanka, Europe…), who need to plan around prayer and halal food. The app is Arabic-first, with English too.

**3. What need does it meet that other apps do not?**
Travel apps sell flights and hotels; prayer apps work only where you are now. Sfrtna puts both together for the trip itself. The day plan is built around the five prayers in the destination's time, Friday prayer and halal meals, and it stays usable offline on the plane and abroad. It also alerts at prayer times in the city being visited.

**4. Beta testing and feedback.**
Friends and family who tried the app and the site this month, our own tests on iPhones through TestFlight, and the Android test track. Examples of feedback and what we changed:
- Prayer boxes overflowed on small phones → redesigned the day layout.
- Arabic letters were clipped in the route banner → fixed text rendering.
- Testers wanted the plan without internet abroad → "Keep on my phone" and offline "My plans".
- Friends said the trip plan was too thin for a long trip (empty days in a small city) → the plan now spreads a long trip across a country's cities, with the days each city needs and the road time between them.
- Friends said the useful parts were hidden behind search pages → the app now opens on its own Today and Plan screens; the plan questions and the plan itself are native screens.
- Our phone tests: "Download PDF" opened the print screen and the plan e-mail did not arrive → the PDF now goes to the iPhone share sheet (Files, WhatsApp) and the e-mail was fixed.

**5. Can the app work on its own?**
Yes. Today, My plans, the qibla, prayer times and alerts all work without an account and without internet. Only the nearby search and building a new plan need a connection.

**6. Could it be an in-app purchase or feature of another app?**
No. It is our only app and our own product.

**7. Does it share code with other apps you made?**
No. This is our only app on the account.

**8. Does it share code, SDKs or content with third-party apps?**
The app uses the open-source Capacitor runtime to show our own city guide pages in one tab ("Guide"). Its native screens are our own Swift code: tabs, the plan questions and the plan view, prayer calculation, qibla, nearby search, offline plans, notifications and the home / lock screen widget. The city guide and plans are generated by our own engine. There is no white-label or partner widget in the iPhone app.

**9. Was it made for a client or with an app-generation service?**
No. We, the owner of this developer account, write and run Sfrtna ourselves. It was not made from a template or an app builder.

Steps to review (also in App Review notes): open the app → Today (allow location) → Plan tab → choose Istanbul, dates, answers → "Build my plan" → "Keep on my phone" → Airplane Mode → My plans → open the plan. Then add the Sfrtna widget to the home or lock screen.
